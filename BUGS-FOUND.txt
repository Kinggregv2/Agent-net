AGENTNET BUGS (kept up to date by Claude: open ones first, then what was fixed)
================================================================================
A bug = any unintended behaviour, including deaths that could have been prevented.
Last updated: 2026-11-19 18:10, from crew runs "run 1118d", "run 1119a" and "run 1119b".

--- OPEN (found, not fixed yet) --------------------------------------------------

O1. THE MAIN SPEEDRUNNER STILL DIES AND LOSES ITS KIT. Every death costs a full regear
    (10 to 30 minutes). Causes seen on 2026-11-19: night mobs in the overworld (skeleton,
    spider, creeper, zombie pack), a piglin + hoglin 26 s after entering the Nether (fix
    for that one written 18:05, not yet seen working), a magma cube on its own lava
    bridge (fix written, not yet seen working), a fall in the Nether onto a block that was
    not there on the server (run 1118d, cause unknown).
O2. IT ENTERED THE NETHER WITHOUT A SHIELD ("shield false" in its nether status) in run
    1119b at 70 min. The kit check before the portal only looks for a pickaxe and a weapon.
O3. SHORT "PUT BACK BY THE SERVER" LOOPS still happen (1 to 3 per agent in 6 minutes; were
    250 at one spot). Two causes were fixed (see F3, F4); what is left is not diagnosed.
O4. MINERS TRY ORE THEY CANNOT REACH for minutes ("skipping iron_ore: every way there is
    blocked", 75 times in 10 minutes for one agent) before moving on.
O5. BUILDER "can't get within reach" of its own block 42 times in 10 minutes.
O6. BOTS SOMETIMES MINE THE BASE (a hunter dug a hole through its own house). Low priority
    (the user: just write it down).
O7. THE DISTRIBUTOR OFTEN DELIVERS NOTHING ("0 collected, 0 delivered" with 7 to 12 jobs
    queued), partly because it digs in for the night like everyone else.
O8. NO TREES NEAR SOME SPAWNS (snowy mountains): the whole crew is short of wood and the

O9. ELYTRA MACE: the fighter read its own Strength as 0 while it had Strength II (so it

    health and our Strength is written but not tested in-game.
O10. Helpers sit "making itself an iron chestplate" for minutes without moving (AFK line).
O11. Old ones from 2026-11-16 that nobody has re-checked: keeper hiring loop (old 2), idle

--- FIXED ON 2026-11-19 (seen working in a run unless it says otherwise) ----------

F1. Helpers never mined: two routines shared one flag and started/stopped the miner every
    tick (8 400 times in 6 minutes). Now they mine and hand iron to the main.
F2. Agent with no pickaxe sealed in a one-block shaft refused to dig at all. Now digs out
    level or upward by hand.
F3. Digs refused by the server as "too fast" (the server ran slower than the agents'
    clocks), so agents walked into blocks that were still there. Dig time now stretches
    with every refusal. 100 refusals in 6 minutes -> 1 or 2.
F4. Walker clipped a block beside the path after being pushed off-centre. It now lines up
    on the centre line first.
F5. Agents dug in for the night forgot it across a restart and sat sealed in their holes.
    They now go back to waiting (or climb out by day); dug-in agents no longer show as AFK.
F6. Shield took 4 minutes every regear: 3 planks + 3 logs, and the craft does not saw logs.
    Now 1 second.
F7. Bucket stage waited on a crafting table it could not reach / had left behind. Now it
    chops logs and makes one.
F8. "Up to the surface" climb restarted every 10 s and never finished. Now a climb under
    way is left alone, and after 6 that gain nothing it searches from where it is.
F9. Back from a death it walked through its finished portal with empty hands. Now it gears
    up first.
F10. Tank helper stood in the Nether portal "holding the blazes" with no blaze and no
    fortress. Now it travels with the group until there is one.
F11. Miner stood on a beach trying 65 stone blocks under water. Now it leaves an area after
    six unreachable targets in a row.
F12. Armed speedrunner always fought at night, even four mobs at once. Now it digs in when
    two or more come, or a creeper.
F13. Rescuer restarted its mining job every 5 s; then the role was removed altogether (the
    user's call): the civ hires none and turns a saved one into a miner.
F14. Chains of elytra-mace smashes were cut off after 60 s while still landing hits; the
    target's helmet was never counted as armor.
F15. A facing-only packet while gliding reset the server's fall count (mace dives that
    counted 4 blocks instead of 9+).
F16. Old 1 (distributor crash on dimension()), fixed 2026-11-16.
    Written, not yet seen working: logs sawn into planks before fuelling a furnace; hitting
    a mob on its lava bridge instead of walking into it; keeping away from hoglins/piglins.

AGENTNET BUG TEST RESULTS (2026-11-16, nothing fixed, only observed)
=====================================================================
Test run: potions, Wind Burst elytra mace, cart PvP, then 9 minutes of autopilot
(cap 12, fresh crew at spawn). Most important first. "Seen" = in the log.

--- BUGS (broke or went wrong in the test) --------------------------------------

1. DISTRIBUTOR CRASHES (NullPointerException on dimension()).
   Seen twice: "distributor module crashed", role removed. Happens right after an agent is
   made a distributor, while its dimension is still unknown (just joined). Every crash
   costs a distributor, which is the logistics backbone.

2. AUTOPILOT HIRES A KEEPER EVERY ROUND AND ENDS UP WITH EXTRA DISTRIBUTORS.
   Seen at 01:12, 01:13, 01:14: "the base's storage has no keeper -> a new keeper joins".
   Final crew: 3 distributors, 0 keepers. Cause: the autopilot counts the old test base at
   -1563 70 1805 as built, but it is ~660 blocks away; the keeper checks "built near me",
   finds nothing and works as a distributor. The two checks disagree.

3. FARMERS SIT IDLE FOREVER.
   Seen: "no free farm and no spot to make one within 40 blocks" for both farmers, and
   they just stood at spawn. The autopilot counted 2 farm areas (set up earlier, far away),
   hired 2 farmers for them, they can't use them (over 256 blocks) and found no water or
   flat ground at spawn to make one. Needs: walk looking for a spot instead of waiting,
   and the autopilot must ignore farm areas that are too far away.

4. FISHERMEN NEVER GET HIRED: the autopilot waits for a spare fishing rod and nothing
   in the crew ever makes one ("no spare fishing rod in the crew or chests"). Fishers now
   find their own water, so the missing rod is the only thing stopping them.

5. HEALING POTIONS ARE NOT USED.
   A warrior at 8/20 HP with two splash healing and two drinkable healing potions never
   used one: it ate instead ("eating to heal up"). Strength splash worked. Cause not found.

6. MANY STUCK AGENTS (about 280 dig-out starts and 400 route failures in 9 minutes).
   Examples: an explorer trying again and again toward the same far chunks
   ("explorer gave up on chunk ..." in a row), a miner and two distributors with
   "no way from ... (60000 spots searched)", a miner "boxed in" 4 times in a row.
   This is the biggest drain on the crew: stuck agents produce nothing.

7. A KILL ORDER FAR AWAY DOES NOTHING.
   "/agent kill X Dum1" with Dum1 about 56 blocks away: the warrior stayed put. Warriors
   only react to players within 32 blocks. (This also meant the Wind Burst and cart tests
   below never started.)

8. A "kill" TASK STAYS AFTER THE ROLE CHANGES: an agent switched from warrior to miner
   still showed task "kill Dum1" and "waiting for /agent mine".

9. THE LEFTOVER 64 GOLDEN APPLES: the potion test warrior ended up carrying 64 golden
   apples that the test never gave it (unexplained; probably a distributor request
   arriving from an earlier run). Low importance.

--- NOT PROPERLY TESTED (test setup was wrong, run again) ------------------------

10. WIND BURST ELYTRA MACE: not tested (target too far, see 7). Still unverified.
11. CART PVP: not tested for the same reason. In its earlier test it also never started.

--- WORKED --------------------------------------------------------------------

- Autopilot rounds: base builder, workers scaled with guards, 3 guards joined, learning
  lines judged jobs ("coal failed", "distributor worked") and showed in the status.
- Strength splash potion, critical-hit jumps, no kicks, no "invalid move" errors, no
  travel reconnects in this run (the earlier reconnect problem didn't show up).

--- POLISH ---------------------------------------------------------------------

12. Autopilot cap: with cap 12 and 9 workers it only got 3 guards; for a real army the
    cap has to be much higher than the worker count (the log says so each round).
13. Explorer is hired at 4 crew members but then gives up on most chunks (item 6).
14. The learning check judged the coal miner after 5 minutes while it was still
    walking to its ore (coal 0 -> 0); longer waits for it would be fairer.
